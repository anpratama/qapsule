Qapsule

Send a message into the future that no quantum computer can open — and nobody can open early.

Contract: 0xfe5ee7ee9c4820144d7c95f5916722bbcbc492cc · QoreChain Diana testnet (chain 9800)

1 · Recipient keys (ML-KEM-1024)

The recipient generates a post-quantum keypair in their own browser. The secret key never leaves their device.

2 · Seal a capsule

Keep the reveal file private until the unlock time — it holds Share B. The capsule file is safe to send to the recipient now.

3 · Open a capsule

4 · Reveal (sender, after unlock)

Testnet demo. Time enforcement lives at the key-reveal layer: the contract refuses to record Share B before the unlock time, and the reveal is verified against the on-chain commitment. QoreChain senders need a registered ML-DSA-87 key and a live EVM authorisation window (see README).
